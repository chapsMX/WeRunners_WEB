The map just got a new pin. **STRNGR Running Community** from **Munich, Germany** is now on We Runners, and their kilometers start counting for their club from today.

## Meet STRNGR

STRNGR runs out of **Munich East and Riemer Park**. They're young: their first session was on October 15, 2026, and they already have **10 runners** on board.

Their idea of running is simple: it's about more than pace and kilometers. They run together, push each other, get better, and have a good time doing it. That's exactly the kind of club this platform was built for.

**Want to run with them?** STRNGR meets every **Tuesday at 7:00 PM** and every **Friday at 7:00 AM**, and all levels are welcome. Find details and the join link on [their website](https://www.strongerwithkatalina.com/strngr-running).

## Why this matters

On We Runners, every runner belongs to one club. That means every kilometer a STRNGR runner logs goes to STRNGR, and only to STRNGR. No splitting your effort across five apps and three groups. Your club, your numbers, one leaderboard.

Ten runners in Munich may sound small. Give it a few weeks of Tuesday evenings and Friday mornings and watch what happens on the board.

Welcome to the hub, STRNGR. 🇩🇪⚡

## Is your club next?

If you run a club, whether it's 10 people or 500, there's a spot for you here. Getting on takes a few minutes:

1. **Sign up** at [app.w3runn3rs.com](https://app.w3runn3rs.com).
2. **Create your club**, or join it if it's already there.
3. **Invite your runners.** Each one picks your club and connects a Garmin or Polar.
4. **Go run.** Every new run counts for your club automatically.

You keep using Strava and everything else the way you do now. We Runners doesn't replace anything; it just gives your club one place where its kilometers add up.

Questions, or want a hand setting up your club? Write to [run@w3runn3rs.com](mailto:run@w3runn3rs.com).
