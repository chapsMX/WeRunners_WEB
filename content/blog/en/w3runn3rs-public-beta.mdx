The first runs are already in. Six running clubs have signed up so far, in Mexico City, Miami, New York, Davao and Astana. Every mile their runners log lands where it belongs: on their club's total.

The W3 Runn3rs public beta is open. Your club can be next.

## What W3 Runn3rs does right now

It takes the runs from your watch and adds them to your club. That's it.

One mile run is one mile for your club. No points, no multipliers, no formulas. Your club's total is the sum of what its runners actually ran.

## How to get started in four steps

1. **Sign up.** Go to [app.w3runn3rs.com](https://app.w3runn3rs.com) and create your account.
2. **Pick your club.** Find your running club in the list and join it. Not there yet? Create it, then drop the link in your club's group chat.
3. **Connect your watch.** Link your Garmin or Polar account.
4. **Go run.** Once your watch syncs, your run shows up and counts for your club.

## One thing to know: only new runs count

Garmin and Polar don't let us pull runs from before you connected. Your history stays on your watch. Your club's total starts with your next run.

So the sooner you connect, the sooner your miles count.

## What "beta" means for you

You'll find rough edges. Some things will change.

The leaderboard that ranks clubs around the world isn't live yet. Right now we're recording every mile, runner by runner and club by club. The ranking is what we're building next.

The clubs in the beta get a real say in how that happens: what a club page shows, how totals read, and what the leaderboard should measure. If something breaks, feels confusing, or you have an idea, write to [run@w3runn3rs.com](mailto:run@w3runn3rs.com). Every email goes straight to the team building it.

Before your next run:

- Open [app.w3runn3rs.com](https://app.w3runn3rs.com)
- Join your club, or create it
- Connect your Garmin or Polar

Then go run. Those miles are your club's now.
