Something is shifting. People are waking up at 5:30am instead of scrolling until midnight. They're booking flights to run marathons in Tokyo. They're choosing race bibs over concert tickets.

Running clubs aren't just groups of people who run together anymore. They're the new social infrastructure.

## Why Running Became a Social Movement

For decades, running was a solitary sport. You laced up, put in your earphones and went. The finish line was personal.

Then something changed.

Strava made runs social. Park Runs made Saturday mornings communal. HYROX turned fitness into a spectator sport. And somewhere in between, running clubs became the thing people *belonged* to.

Today, a running club isn't just a training group — it's an identity. Your club is your crew, your social circle, your travel companions and your competition.
