Not every runner has a club.

Maybe you run alone at 6 a.m. because that's the only hour that's yours. Maybe you just moved to a new city and haven't found your people yet. Or maybe you do Tuesday intervals with one club and Sunday long runs with another, and picking just one feels like choosing a favorite kid.

We Runners runs on one simple rule: **one runner, one club.** Every mile you run counts for yours. That rule is what makes a club's total mean something.

But it left a gap. What about the runners who haven't picked a club yet?

That's why we built the **Solo Running Club**.

## A club for runners without a club

The Solo Running Club works like any other club on We Runners. You join, connect your watch, and your miles start counting from your next run.

You don't have to wait until you've found the "right" club to be part of We Runners. You can start today.

## The difference: you can leave anytime

When you join a club on We Runners, you stay with it for 6 months before you can switch. That keeps things fair: nobody jumps to whichever club is having a big week.

The Solo Running Club is the only club without that lock. Join today, and the day you find your club, you move. No waiting period.

![A runner alone on an empty avenue at sunrise](/blog/soloPost_01.jpg)

One thing to know: once you join a regular club, its 6-month rule applies. So take your time. Run with a few groups, try their Saturday long run, see who shows up when it's raining. When you're sure, make the switch. (Not sure what to look for? Here's [how to choose your running club](/en/blog/how-to-choose-your-running-club).)

## Who it's for

- **You run solo.** And you like it that way, at least for now.
- **You're new.** New to running, or new to town, and still looking.
- **You run with more than one club.** You haven't decided which one gets your miles.
- **Your club isn't on We Runners yet.** Count your miles here while you get your crew to sign up, or create the club yourself.

## How to join

1. Go to the [Solo Running Club page](https://app.w3runn3rs.com/clubs/solo-running-club/join) and sign in with Google or your email.
2. Join the club.
3. Connect your Garmin or Polar.
4. Go run.

Heads up: only runs recorded after you connect your device count. Past runs can't be imported, so connect before your next run, not after.

## Your club is out there

The Solo Running Club isn't meant to be forever. It's where your miles count while you look for the club that's actually yours.

Pick your running club, make it count. And if you haven't picked one yet, start here.
